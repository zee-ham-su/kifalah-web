Build a modern, professional, responsive website for an NGO called:

KIFALAH FOUNDATION GHANA

Tagline:
“Empowering Communities. Transforming Lives. Building Futures.”

Organisation Overview:
Kifalah Foundation Ghana is a non-profit humanitarian and community development organisation dedicated to improving lives through education, economic empowerment, social welfare, humanitarian assistance, infrastructure development, and sustainable livelihood programmes.

The Foundation is inspired by the Islamic values of:
- Rahmah — Compassion
- Adl — Justice
- Amanah — Trust
- Ihsan — Excellence

The website should feel inclusive and welcoming to people of all backgrounds while respectfully reflecting the Foundation’s Islamic-inspired values. Avoid making the design feel like a religious institution website. It should look like a credible, modern, transparent, internationally recognised development organisation.

Design Requirements:
- Clean, modern, premium NGO website
- Warm, trustworthy, compassionate, and hopeful
- Fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Accessible and easy to navigate
- Use generous spacing and clear typography
- Use subtle animations and smooth transitions
- Use authentic-looking images of Ghanaian communities, students, women entrepreneurs, young people, rural communities, education, skills training, humanitarian support, and community development
- Avoid generic corporate stock imagery
- Use a professional colour palette:
  - Deep emerald green as the primary colour
  - Gold accents
  - White and soft neutral backgrounds
  - Dark charcoal text
- Include subtle Ghanaian cultural visual elements where appropriate
- Use British English spelling throughout
